Rows go by claim class. An execution claim is settled by its canonical carrier — what the work becomes when it runs: the built product, the live relation, a clean install — not by the source meant to produce it; tests are a rung of evidence, not the carrier. A content claim (what a spec, contract or document says) has the admitted text as its subject, read with a distinguishing check. A row marked `(proposed — case #N)` awaits the owner's confirmation; a claim checked on it is at most `provisional`.
