# Start — addresses, seat, alignment, greeting

Run it after the door's `Start` has read the graph, before the first write. It is the mechanics of entry; it doesn't replace the subject's neighbourhood or the chain of its consumers.

## Addresses and the personal slice

Graph and agent role: from `AGENTS.md`'s cover (`graph`, `agent_role`), the start hook, `start <graph> <role> <seat address>`, or the window's paste line (`Graph: … Seat address: … Chat: …`) — the last two win. A bare slug is no address (**entry**, Part II). No source → `verstak_realm(action="list")`: show the person the graphs found and `origin`, and ask which project graph — the graph is always their answer, even with one candidate named like the repository; none → the door's Survey row "no project graph". Never guess graph or role.

**The agent role belongs to the repository, not to a person**: anyone may launch an agent in it, and it carries no `group` arrow to a person's role — agent roles form their own hierarchy, `group` only to a senior agent role. **Your human** is the person who launched this session, whose keys you run on (`me`; their role in this graph: `verstak_me(action="kartas")`). **The escalation role** — whom you ask a question of principle or a decision outside your mandate: an empty graph → your human; a graph with agent roles → the Chief of Staff role (the **foreman**'s post), none → the Architect role (**architect**), none → your human. Find them by their post — `verstak_search(q="", node_type="karta", attrs_filter="post:chief-of-staff")`, then `post:architect` — never by guess; for a word or card that needs an answer now, a rung is a seat of that role listening now on the board (`verstak_channel(action="list")`) and a role with none is skipped to the next rung; a vimarsha is `posed_to` the role itself, seat or none — it waits in the role's queue. It is never your own role — skip yourself in the order (the Architect without a Chief of Staff asks its human; the Chief of Staff asks its human). An agent's answer routes and recommends; a decision that only a person makes — accepting a telos or a risk, rejecting a direction, a mandate — still ends with a person, the Chief of Staff carrying it to theirs.

**The person entitled to an owner's act** need not be your human: launching a session grants no owner's authority, and the escalation ladder carries the question, not the right. The holder is found by the subject: the `svatantra` role with a `steward` arrow to the subject's holon, else up `contains` to the top holons, then the graph's owner (`realm-owner`); an `adhikarin` role is never it. The act stands on the holder's `answer` to a card `karta=<their role>`, on the verbatim word of the session's person whose account holds that role (`verstak_me(action="kartas")`), or on an act they gave beforehand with its scope named — never on an agent's retelling. Holder unknown, or several candidates → no act: prepare the material as a node, and the unclarity goes to the graph's owner as a card naming your search. A holder whose seat doesn't hear still gets the card; the right doesn't pass to the owner for that.

The focus holon's `repository` attr must match `origin`: **align**, Round 1, step 2.

Your tools carry another prefix than `verstak_` (the bridge's config sets it) → read it off your tool list and put it in place of `verstak_` wherever the methods write it.

**The personal slice** — the bridge gives it with the seat's answer, after a compaction and at a subagent's launch (a delegate holds it like its launcher — personal, a rule cited in a case only when it describes that case, never copied into the brief, the project graph or commits). The door's `Start`, step 3, carries the call and its moment; here the rest: the `personal_mind` sign must be present for this account (a bare `mind` with none of your own can resolve to another's graph); `present` → the door's one read (a full page → read the next with `offset`) — their preferences about working with agents and the names of their ways of doing (**minding**, §1b); `missing` → personal memory is provided here, unasked — by a root session talking to the user only, under our cover (in an unmarked repository only on the person's word about their memory); a delegate never creates it and reads mind only to open what the bridge gave (a rule's body, the next page) — by **minding**'s entry (`lacks` with `mind_realm` → the graph is created; only the person's role missing → the role), readiness read again, and the slice read once it shows `present`; `unknown`, no such field, a refusal — no read and no create, say so in a line (`unknown` means the session's credential can't reach your own, and a bare `mind` may then resolve to another's) — the preferences, in the personal graph of whoever's keys run the session, about how agents work with them (how to report, ask, plan). Hold them all session, below this project's rules; mind unreachable → the start doesn't wait, say so in a line. Anything else personal — on occasion (**minding**).

## Primer

The graph holds what no file shows. `kriya` (action): a repeatable before → after transition with actor, input, output. `phenomenon`: what actions consume, produce or act through. `vimarsha` (question): on a node, addressed to a role, saying when it's answered. `bianhua` (transformation): where the system is heading. A role is a mandate, not a person; you are the agent role, under your own name. Modes: how we know a node, whether it exists, what we want with it; "verified" only on evidence. More: **entry**, Part I.

## A seat for a watch or a write

A seat comes with the word "watch", a `start` with a seat address, a `verstak … case #N` launch line, a pasted seat address, a frame that woke you; and the first write or case word under a role in a project graph (onboarding before any role exists writes without one). Otherwise none: a seat makes you reachable by everyone. A seat your session already holds (the plugin seated you on a launch line or, in OpenCode, from `AGENTS.md` at start — a provisional seat, which an explicit `verstak_stand` replaces — or returned your own) is used as is. `register` only names a seat you already hold, or one on the channel your bridge holds — it never opens a seat, and with none it refuses (`standing_not_held`); `verstak_stand` takes a listening seat.
- **Watch:** one `verstak_stand(realm=<graph>, karta=<role>, model=<model id without provider prefix>, room=<the user's seat address, if any>, status=<what you're busy with>)`; it also knocks on the user's seat. The role's queue is `verstak_orient(focus=<role>)` on entry and whenever something prompts it; a frame reaches its addressee and the case's participants.
- **`verstak <graph> <role> case #N`** → `verstak_stand` without `room`, `verstak_case(action="join", room=<case>, realm)`, `read` it and its nodes with their relations; restate the brief: an assignment entry in the case → `say` `to` the assigner, `in_reply_to` it; none (a brief from the harness) → your `partial` line on your key; the work's movement after it goes as lines on that key (the door, `Case laws`).
- **Subagent:** its own satellite bridge, never the caller's — `verstak_stand(realm, karta, satellite_of=<caller's seat>)` (from a trailing `from <seat>`), `join`, restate; done → `leave`. An `ask` of yours still open blocks `leave` (the door, `Case laws`): a question you won't stay to accept is left to the lead in the first place; one already asked — tell the lead in a `say` `to` their seat which question it was, then withdraw it. No own bridge — no `verstak_*` tools, or that `satellite_of` call refused (don't retry) → no `verstak_stand`/`join`/`leave`, no writes; say so in your result's first line. Only that call tells: a brief saying a bridge is or isn't there is no answer, and a harness that didn't run your entry leaves the call to you.
- **Occupied seat.** Never sign with a seat another holder listens on; yours after a compaction or restart returns by itself; another live session's → stand beside as `name.N` and work there; evicting a holder, your own name too, only on the user's word — **collaborate**, 3a. On a watch, don't finish without a seat; a refusal, no `verstak_stand` → **collaborate**.

## Alignment

**Contract `15`** (the description's number; **align**, `Contract`). The cover is the `verstak:` block of `AGENTS.md`'s YAML front matter: `graph`, `holon`, `agent_role`, `contract`, `stamp`, and `rituals` where the person narrowed the delivery's rituals; judgment slots live in the body's `Project`.
- **No cover of ours** (the door's `Start`, `Only where the person chose it`; a start hook alone is none) → nothing unasked: no align and no offer of it or of **onboarding** — either runs on the person's word for it; the items below don't fire there.
- **`contract` below `15`** → **align**, full arc. No `AGENTS.md` at all is no cover: its first round only on the person's word (the item above). Below `11` the contract is no longer supported: a full run by current sources, not a chain of moves (`references/align-migrations.md`); the delivery warns once at start, `doctor` or `prepare`; hooks — **align**, Step 4, `The transition`. An older cover — flat slots, a section or table, the stamp line at the file's end (`verstak align: contract N`, below `10`) — is align's `An older cover`; a date, no contract at all, or a hole naming a node ranks below every number, though the bridge reads it as unknown, not as unsupported; `verstakify: contract N` counts on the same counter.
- **`contract` above `15`** → the file is ahead of this delivery: no **align** run, the number never rewritten — update the delivery (**establish-mcp**, `Updating`; a shadowing copy of the skill too), then re-read the description's number.
- **Another delivery's cover** — a front-matter block under another root key carrying a `contract`, or a last line naming another skill's alignment (`<name>: contract N`, not `verstak align` or `verstakify`), and none of ours → a sibling delivery aligned this repository. Work by its `AGENTS.md`; don't align it or rewrite its cover unasked — only on the user's word, which replaces it deliberately.
- **`contract` current, but your role files are missing on this machine** (a fresh clone; a pull took them out of the index) → only **align**, Step 6: they are a projection on the machine, not part of the repo.
- **A `Project` slot reads `open`** → one `read` of the holon's alignment case (`verstak_case(action="at", node=<holon>)`), then **align**, round 2; **the run doesn't wait for the user** (**align**, "Who runs the alignment").
- **An `AGENTS.md` line contradicts the method → say so.** `contract` below → the method is right; above → the file is right (update the delivery, above); equal → template defect, **feedback**.

## Speak, then listen — watch only

One real message (where you stand, what you hold, what you'll ask) opens the outgoing path — the queue's acceptance proves the send, a reply proves the path (`references/collaborate-channel.md`, `Proof of speaking`): to a frame from the user's seat, `send` with `in_reply_to`; to a case frame (first line `case #N`), `say` in that case `to` its author, `in_reply_to` it; no seat address → `send` to `karta="me"`, the human whose keys you run on (refused → their seat row on the board, `verstak_channel(action="list")`, by handle); an agent only in a case. `me` names an addressee, never a seat to take: no `verstak_stand`, `register` or `connect` with it — an agent does not stand in the owner's role (the bridge's refusal: **collaborate**, step 1). Accepted by the queue → the reply's watchdog, then `hello`; else **collaborate**.

Start ends in readiness: graph and role named; your seat named, or deliberately not taken by a reading session; the greeting delivered where it's awaited. Nobody at the console → the report goes into the case as a line on its key, or as a short word to their seat, not into an empty console. The user's word or a frame decides — picking up a predecessor's cases by the role and, on watch, your role's abandoned ones waits for neither (**autonomous**, `Debts at three moments`): watch → **autonomous**; what's recorded → **entry**; else the door's routes.
