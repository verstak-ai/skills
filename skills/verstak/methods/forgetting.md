# forgetting — taking a word of rejection and letting go by kind

**Use when:** the user says something that may drop a direction, an artifact or a thesis — "this whole direction is nonsense", "forget it", "drop this", "we're not doing that"; you meet a record, a branch or a document that has probably lost its point while the knowledge isn't firm or someone else holds the subject. Not `inquiry` (one question's fate), `weaving` (completing what stands), `reconcile` (code against graph after work). Composes `methods/writing.md` with `references/writing-modes.md`, "Three fates of a record no longer held"; `methods/integrity.md`; `methods/lead.md`.

**Rejection is the owner's act.** You don't perform it: you take it and record it by their word — their words go into the act's `reasoning`. "Nonsense" is almost never one thing: under one word stand a will dropping a direction, a false thesis, the outdated, a crude form of something right, scaffolding no longer needed — each with its own move. Dropping a direction refutes none of the facts found along it. **Order matters**: keep the meaning and rebind what depended on it first, then let go — the form is forgotten once its meaning is caught, not erased together with what it taught.

## Taking a word of rejection — seven steps

1. **Read the word's force** by the speaker, the circumstance and what they began and ended with: the same words are refusal, doubt, annoyance, a probe. First recall a decision they already gave on the same subject (the door, `Waiting on an answer`). Not a refusal → the work goes where the word calls.
2. **Outline the subject**: the nodes, branches, documents and directions under the word, with their holders and modes — semantic search on the word's subject, the neighbourhood of what you find, the cases on it (`verstak_case(action="at", node=<node>)`). Name the search's ceiling; a part you can't tell belongs is named unknown, not attached by a guess.
3. **Tell the grounds apart.** Per part: its kind of record, the ground for revisiting it (will, thesis, time, form, schema, harm) and the evidence; parts with different grounds are split even when the user named them with one word. Traps: the outdated taken for refuted, and a culprit sought; the owner's dropping of a direction taken for proof that a thesis is false — will is not knowledge; a right thing in crude form released instead of rewritten; something that did harm released instead of recorded as an incident.
4. **Act and retell after, or ask now.** Ask — an `ask` card in the case with the split ready and a recommendation, never "what did you mean" — when an essential uncertainty of subject, force, authority or consequence changes the admissible move: even with one reading, when the move is irreversible or touches someone else's without firm knowledge. Recoverability is measured by consequences, not by the record: a released node comes back, consumers' stopped work doesn't. Otherwise carry out the grounded part within your mandate and retell after.
5. **Keep the meaning** without which the next agent rederives what was rejected or loses what was found on the way: a link to an existing node, a new distinction, or a "we don't do it this way" rule with `arose_from` to the rejected (`methods/writing.md`). Nothing to teach (noise, a duplicate) → no new node, and say so. For a schema the meaning is kept before the form goes (`references/writing-modes.md`, "Keep the meaning before letting the form go").
6. **Rebind the wave before changing anything** (`methods/integrity.md`): what applied the rejected moves to the kept meaning or to what replaces it; what only referred to it gets a question to its holder about integration; the untouched are established untouched. After the act, check that the rebinding holds.
7. **Carry it out by kind.** Each part moves by its kind of record and its ground — released, refuted and kept, rewritten, or replaced with a link (`references/writing-modes.md`, "Three fates of a record no longer held"); harm becomes an incident; the owner's word stands in the act's `reasoning`. The mode's move follows the kind and the act, not the ground alone. Someone else's, without firm knowledge → a forgetting case before any change (below); what the user surely rejected is carried out at once, someone else's included (the door, `Cross-cutting norms`, across a holon's boundary).

Then **tell the user** in their words: what was released, what refuted, what meaning kept, what waits on its holders, who was rebound, where everything lies and the check's ceiling. A correction is one line, and it reopens the part it touches.

## A forgetting case — when the knowledge isn't firm

You see that a record or a carrier has probably lost its point, but the knowledge isn't firm, or another holds the subject. No new mechanism: an ordinary case on the subject node. Open it there (`open_room`), bind every touched node to it, and invite the holders — the stewards of every touched holon; nodes chained across several holons → all their stewards. That is the appeal to the community. Say what you propose to forget, on what sign, and what would refute "outdated". Close it by `methods/lead.md`, `Closing`: an objection stops it; silence is consent only of whoever received it and could speak; what nobody defended is released, what was defended stands with its named ground. The procedure supplies no required sanction: where releasing needs the owner's act, the proposal names that act as missing. Material things — a branch, a worktree, a document — go by the same kind of case on their holon's node, with its steward.

**No case when the knowledge is firm**: your own record you release yourself; what the user surely rejected is carried out at once (step 7). A one-off question of a case is no forgetting case either: it ends in its case.
