# inquiry — the life of a vimarsha and the field of inquiry

**Use when:** an answer, merge or decision lands on a question → end it by axis; orient shows hanging vimarshas; a session ends. A vimarsha's life, not its posing (`methods/writing.md`): anchor, resolve, end, accept as is, supersede, crystallize, attach to a transformation. Phrases: "go through the vimarshas", "close / keep this question", "triage the questions". Out of failure 4 (the door, `The method fails in five ways`). Not a pass over the whole graph (`methods/assembly.md`).

A vimarsha — an open question — records a *tension with reality* to be discharged: resolved, released, or crystallized into a form. Graphs pile up **hanging** vimarshas: unanchored, unresolved, invisible to whoever could discharge them.

## An assignment is not a vimarsha

A one-off task lives as a message in a case and ends there with an outcome, not with `visarjana` (→ door, `One-off task`). A vimarsha that is a task underneath is a reconciliation debt: record what the task changes as nodes, hand what's unfinished as an assignment in a case on the subject's node, and release the vimarsha with a reasoning line (`methods/reconcile.md`, pass 2).

## 1. Anchoring is mandatory

An unanchored vimarsha appears in no `lens="vimarshas"` and reaches no node. Every vimarsha needs **`vimarsha_of`** → the node(s) it is *about*, and **`arose_from`** → the observation it was *seen from* (a node, an incident, an earlier vimarsha).

A vimarsha carrying an **expectation** (`posed_to` a role, `anga` to a transformation) needs its anchor twice over: agents find work by orienting on a holon, and neither `anga` nor `posed_to` puts it in one. Anchor at least to the holon where the work lives, better to the exact node — when you pose; the `unanchored` detector fires only afterwards. Waiting on someone → `posed_to` the `adhikarin` stewarding the work's holon (the `steward` arrow or `verstak_search(q="", node_type="karta")`, not orient's partial list of root roles). Address rules and the mirror failures: `methods/writing.md`, Decision 5.

Hanging vimarsha in orient: anchor first, then triage.

## 2. Lifecycle — three exits

A vimarsha stays in work while it calls: a doubt *seeking an answer*, a risk *guarding*, a defect *present*; *holding the question* and *accepted* stand without calling. It leaves by exactly one door:

| Exit | What happens | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Resolution** | answered by an acting or deciding node | `addressed_by` + volitive `visarjana`. A behavioural answer ("works", "fixed") needs a reality check at a declared level first (`methods/reality-audit.md`) |
| **Death** | released *without* an answer — wrong, fell away, superseded | `visarjana` with a reasoning line saying *why* |
| **Crystallization** — the most valuable | the insight sets into a standing form (a principle, distinction, method) that keeps being applied | a phenomenon with a `given_as`, `arose_from` → the vimarsha, `addressed_by` vimarsha → it; `visarjana` once the form carries the content |

Two non-terminal moves:
- **Supersession** — a later question absorbs this one: `supersedes` on the replacement, the old one → `visarjana`.
- **Accepted as is** — a deliberate volitive `upeksha`: seen and standing, an answer neither pursued nor refused. For a doubt, counter-thesis or hint it's your move, with reasoning; for the unwanted (risk, defect, incident), the owner's (§5). A mode, never a return date in `attrs`. It wakes when its anchor changes (new mode, arrow or vimarsha there): editing an anchor, ask whether that touches the question; if so, back to `chanda`. Accepted is **not answered**: off the queue, but not a resolved part of a transformation. With no anchor that could change, it is quiet forgetting — release it.

**A refuted question is not ended.** `badhita` on a doubt or risk means posed wrongly or impossible — reformulate, supersede or release; will does the ending.

**Moves are modes and arrows.** `verstak_update(... volitive_mode=...)` moves a mode; `verstak_arrow(action="link", ...)` adds `addressed_by` / `supersedes` / `arose_from`. What can answer, and why an arrow ends nothing: `methods/writing.md`, Decision 5. Which ends the surface accepts: `verstak_arrow(action="?")`, not memory — it can be narrower than the canon; a refusal states its reason. Set terminal modes in one update; `update` / `delete` / `reconnect` need `basis_version` from a fresh `verstak_look`.

## 3. The genre sets what discharges it — and which axis ends it

| Genre | Discharged by | Ending axis |
|---|---|---|
| `samshaya` (doubt) | removing the uncertainty — a decision, a found node, a check with its level named (graph or world) | will: `visarjana` or `virodha` (refusal); `addressed_by` doesn't end it; `upeksha` = accepted as is |
| `prati-paksha` (counter-thesis) | an evaluative decision: accepted, transformed or declined | will: as `samshaya` |
| `hint` (a transformation's seed) | edited in place to what stands now, each agent clearing what it finished; ended by the coordinator at acceptance | will: as `samshaya` |
| `risk` | a mitigating kriya, a `grundsatz` invariant, or acceptance — the owner's decision (prepare, address, record what they said). `addressed_by` / `realized_as` are informational: a risk that happened (`realized_as` → the state of affairs) is not ended, and is worse than an open one | will: live in `virodha`; only `visarjana` ends it; `upeksha` = accepted, alive as a record |
| `vyabhichara` (counterexample) | a repair that removes the defect, or a refined rule | ontic: present while `vartamana`; ended by `atita` (fixed), `vikalpa` (never was — only with `badhita`), `nashta` (gone with its carrier) or `visarjana`; `upeksha` at `vartamana` = accepted (owner's decision) |
| `hetu-dosha` (grounding defect) | correcting or withdrawing the argument | ontic: as `vyabhichara` |
| `semantic-drift` | re-fixing the term (正名, rectified names) and reconciling the diverged uses — not agreeing the question went away | ontic: as `vyabhichara` |

If you can't say what would discharge a vimarsha, its genre may be wrong — re-check with `methods/writing.md`.

**Accepted is neither released nor calling.** A risk, defect or drift in `upeksha` is finished as work, alive as a record, counted on an "accepted" surface (who, growth rate, actions through it — as the surface shows today). "Done" differs: a repair moved the defect to `atita`. An incident you live with is accepted through its owning question (a defect vimarsha or a realized risk); a bare state of affairs in `upeksha` says nothing.

## 4. Inquiry and transformation (bianhua)

Decide deliberately: is the vimarsha part of a **bianhua** (a qualitative transformation under way) or a **free question**?

- **`anga` of a transformation** — its resolution moves it: `verstak_arrow(action="link", arrow_type="anga", source=<vimarsha>, target=<bianhua>)`; it keeps its own `vimarsha_of`. The genre sets the contribution: a doubt removes a blocking uncertainty, a risk protects, a counterexample fixes a defect, a counter-thesis decision moves it. Attach to the existing forest (`lens="bianhua"`); one vimarsha never justifies a new transformation — none fits → ask the user.
- **Free** — a genuine open question, left free on purpose.

`verstak_search(anga_of=<bianhua>)` also shows **carrier kriyas** (the *arrival*, beside the vimarshas' *path*); what counts as progress is the surface's response, not this text. Clustering the field is assembly's job; this method decides each vimarsha's fate.

## 5. Escalation

| You decide (alongside weaving) | You put on the owner's agenda |
|---|---|
| anchor a hanging vimarsha | decline a proposal |
| end an answered question | prioritise between questions |
| leave it as is, with deliberate reasoning | choose otherwise against a recorded direction |
| attach a driver to a transformation | accept the unwanted — risk, defect, incident |

The right column carries the owner's will: escalate, don't resolve it to clear the field. The vimarsha stays `posed_to` the owner role; a choice the work waits on now also goes as an `ask` card in the subject's case, one per vimarsha (`methods/collaborate.md`, `Asking the user`). Their `answer` → `ack`, then write: their stance as the vimarsha's mode, who said it and when in `reasoning`.

## 6. A session must arrive at understanding

A session must converge on emerging patterns of understanding (`bildung`), not multiply vimarshas; a triage leaving more questions calling and nothing crystallized hasn't landed. Before closing, ask *what do we understand now that we didn't?* — and crystallize it: vimarshas and incidents → thesis, kriya pattern, principle; `visarjana` is a by-product.

## Acceptance

Every triage outcome is exactly one move from §§2–5: anchor, resolve, release, crystallize, supersede, accept as is, attach as anga, escalate. Next moment: a transformation's parts moved → reread its map (`lens="bianhua"`).
