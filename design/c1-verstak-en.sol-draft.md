## 1. Decision in brief

Project C1 into three moments of action, not nineteen questions on every turn:

1. **Identify the claim before grading its evidence.** Replace source-kind grading and automatic graph-agreement escalation with claim/subject/means/ceiling distinctions. Textual acceptance is legitimate; execution needs its execution carrier. Proposed carriers permit investigation without acquiring canonical status.
2. **Choose the carrier of work by its kind and the right to act by its actual sanction.** Keep substantive questions in the graph, one-off assignments and questions in the case, and transport on the channel. Understanding, receipt, acknowledgment, procedure closure and owner sanction are distinct. Keep the English delivery's product-specific rules explicitly product-specific.
3. **Preserve the ability to continue judgment.** Repair lifecycles at the subject's level without destroying retained participants; preserve grounds, scope, counterexamples and unanswered questions without compulsory lessons, seeds, crystals or numerical decomposition. Use lenses by the question, not an obligatory fan of calls.

Shares: **#931** owns English skills, references/templates and bridge/plugin words. **#163** owns the language-independent server tool contract and its ru/en expressions. No new node type, mode axis, runtime-instance layer, or mandatory per-turn dashboard is proposed.
