# Modes — self-check, patterns, cell words, moves

Read by the `writing` method (**writing**, Decision 3) when a triad is in doubt, when you need to name a node's state, or when a mode change is refused or flagged. The factory descriptions carry each value and question; this file holds what they don't.

## The nests behind the values

- **Epistemic** (means of valid knowledge): `kalpita` — built by the mind, a hypothesis; `anumita` — inference (smoke, so fire; reading a function, so its behaviour); `pratyakshita` — "before the eyes" (curl saw the response); `pramanita` — attested on independent grounds; retellings of one source count once; `badhita` — defeat of a thesis in debate: an active refutation, kept, not deleted.
- **Ontic** (what is known): `vartamana` — "under way", functioning, not "alive"; `anagata` — "not yet arrived": a plan, risk, goal; `atita` — "gone beyond", left traces; `vikalpa` — a construct with no external correlate, held for thought; `nashta` — perished without trace (rare: a record in the graph is itself a trace).
- **Volitive**: `chanda` — aspiration toward the wanted; `adhimoksha` — resolve holding it in place; `upeksha` — "to look closely without interfering": considered non-interference; `virodha` — opposition, polarised by kind; `visarjana` — the holders step out of the field.

## Self-check

Read the triad as a sentence: "We [epistemic] this, it [ontic], and we [volitive] it." Abbreviations: Kl `kalpita`, An `anumita`, Pt `pratyakshita`, Pm `pramanita`, Ba `badhita`; Va `vartamana`, Ag `anagata`, At `atita`, Vk `vikalpa`, Na `nashta`; Ch `chanda`, Ad `adhimoksha`, Up `upeksha`, Vi `virodha`, Vs `visarjana`.

✓ "We *observed* this, it *is in effect*, and we *accept* it." → Pt/Va/Up.
✗ "We *inferred* this, it *doesn't exist yet*, and we *accept* it." → An/Ag/Up — designed the future and don't care? Likely An/Ag/Ch.

`chanda` vs `upeksha` (on a `vartamana` or `anagata` state or action the will isn't against): **held by intent or by mechanism?** Arises or continues by itself → `upeksha`; someone works for it → `chanda`. Not asked on `vikalpa`, on the Vi/Ad/Vs patterns, and untested on roles and ideal phenomena.

## Cell words — how to name a node's state

Name a state with the cell word of the kind's **carrying axis**, not "open", "closed", "alive", "active". A kind is type × `given_as` × genre. Axis words: ontic — ahead, in effect, past, construct, lost; epistemic — supposed, inferred, observed, attested, refuted; volitive — wanted, held, accepted, rejected, released.

Reading order: (1) `visarjana` on any kind → **released**; a released vimarsha with an answer arrow → **resolved**. (2) The kind's closing will: doubt, counter-thesis, hint, seed → **declined**; bianhua → **rejected**; principle → **repealed**. (3) The carrying axis's cell.

| Carrying axis | Kinds | Cell → word (kind idiom) |
|---|---|---|
| ontic | kriya, method, role, holon, thing, state of affairs, counterexample, grounding defect, drift, bianhua | `anagata` **ahead**; `vartamana` **in effect** (kriya, method — practised; role — performed; thing, state, defect — present; bianhua — under way); `atita` **past** (kriya, method, role — retired; defect — fixed; bianhua — accomplished); `vikalpa` **construct** (with refutation — never was); `nashta` **lost** |
| epistemic | seed, concept | **supposed**, **inferred**, **observed**, **attested** (seed — time to crystallize), **refuted** (seed — did not emerge) |
| volitive | doubt, counter-thesis, hint | `chanda` **seeking an answer**; `adhimoksha` **holding the question**; `upeksha` **accepted**; `virodha` **declined** |
| volitive | risk | `virodha` **guarding** (· happened); `upeksha` **accepted**; others — axis word · not native to this kind |
| volitive | principle | `chanda` **proposed**; `adhimoksha` **in force**; `upeksha` **in force as given**; `virodha` **repealed** |

Qualifiers after the dot: "· accepted" (present · accepted), "· being phased out", "· opposed" (an unwanted state); a bianhua always states its will ("· wanted", "· held"); a principle — "in force · grounds fallen"; a concept or principle — "· historical"; an unknown cell — the axis word "· not native to this kind".

Calls for action: **seeking an answer**, **guarding**, **present** on a defect, **ahead** with will wanted or held, **proposed** (owner's decision), **attested** on a seed, **supposed** (check it). Doesn't call: **holding the question**, **accepted**, **in force**, **practised**, **past**, **lost**. Machine tokens are read from the surface's response.

## Polarity of virodha and ending

Canon of kinds; a divergence from the surface — as in `Forbidden triads`. `verstak_look` glosses the carrying axis on every node.

The table by kind lives in **writing**, Decision 3. Beyond it: the epistemic axis ends only seed and concept, by `badhita`; `visarjana` always ends, shown apart from a carrying-axis ending.

**Refutation: was it ever so?** `badhita` is an epistemic act: any doer with refuting grounds on the carrier sets it, naming them in `reasoning`; the will moves on someone else's record (release, accept, refuse) are its holder's or the rightful principal's — to another, a word to the holder. The surface rejects `badhita` on `vartamana`; the answer moves the ontic: was and no longer is → `atita`; never was → `vikalpa` (a move into `vikalpa` from `vartamana` or `atita` only with `badhita`; a theoretical construct is born `vikalpa`). `atita` → `vikalpa` rewrites history: its ground is evidence that the being never was, not the absence of a trace. The refutation itself proved false → out of `vikalpa`: epistemic to `anumita`, the ontic again by "was it ever so?". Doubt without refutation → epistemic to An/Kl, the ontic stands. These moves are legitimate as a **reverify event** — a mode touch with new grounds in `reasoning`. On ontically carried kinds what closes is not the refutation itself but the ontic move it forces.

**Three answers to the unwanted:** `virodha` rejects (work); `upeksha` accepts (a fact, out of open work, counted on its own "accepted" surface); `visarjana` releases. Done differs from accepted ontically: done moved to `atita`/`vikalpa` by an action; accepted still stands. An incident is accepted through its owning risk or defect vimarsha; the state of affairs follows. On a doubt, counter-thesis or hint, `upeksha` = accepted as is: out of the queue, woken by a change at its anchor (not a date), not counted as resolved.

**Principle:** `chanda` proposed (born so at an agent's hand); `adhimoksha` in force, by the owner; `upeksha` given. Grounds are `derived_from`/`arose_from`, not a field; missing → tension. `badhita` doesn't end a held principle: name living grounds or release it. Applied-ness is read from incoming `upadhi`, not the mode.

**Misread as endings:** `pramanita` on a seed (crystallize); `atita` on a concept or principle (historical; not counted as orphan); `upeksha` on a bianhua (kind error: history or a thought).

## Moves between triads

Forward along the carrier and rising confidence (Kl → An → Pt → Pm) are legitimate acts.

1. **Intent → in effect:** An/Ag/Ch → Pt/Va, will reconsidered: mechanism → Up, intent → Ch.
2. **Intent → abandoned:** → An/Ag/Vs. What never came about doesn't become past.
3. **Risk → incident:** Kl/Ag/Vi → Pt/At/Vi, by modes or `realized_as` → `sachverhalt`. The risk isn't ended: `visarjana` ends it, `upeksha` accepts it.
4. **Incident → released:** Pt/At/Vi → Pt/At/Vs. The answer arrow releases nothing — the holder does.
5. **Observation → attested:** Pt → Pm only with new evidence at a declared level; for behaviour, on the canonical carrier, not the graph.
6. **Attested → refuted:** Pm/Va → Ba/At or Ba/Vk — by "was it ever so?" (above).
7. **Phased out → retired:** Pt/Va/Vi → Pt/At/Vs.
8. **Unwanted → accepted:** Vi → Up, ontic untouched — the owner's act. A kriya consuming the defect moves it to At instead.
9. **Vimarsha → accepted as is:** Ch/Ad → Up; when its anchor changes, back to the will it is held in while open — Ch for a doubt, counter-thesis or hint, Vi for a risk, counterexample, grounding defect or drift (**inquiry**, §2).
10. **Proposed → in force (principle) or accepted (transformation):** Ch → Ad — the owner's act.
11. **Stub → intent:** Up → Ch or Ad.
12. **Loss:** Va → Na, At → Na — ontically carried kinds only.

**Suspicious** (flagged; fix by the mode, not by restructuring): resurrection (`atita` → `vartamana`, `nashta` → anything) without grounds; Pm → An/Kl without a reverify event; `anagata` → `atita`/`nashta` skipping `vartamana` (for a risk, Ag → At is realisation); `atita` → `vikalpa` and out of `vikalpa` — always shown, legitimate with grounds ("was it ever so?" above). `visarjana` → `chanda` is a legitimate, logged reopening.

## Three fates of a record no longer held

Probe: *will a future reader learn from this node standing refuted?* Yes — the record carried a false thesis → **refute and keep** (`badhita`, `vikalpa` if being is refuted): it teaches how not to think. Nothing to learn — there was no distinction (empty concept, question about nothing, duplicate, stray) → **delete**, reason in `reasoning`; history keeps the deleted node, restorable, its arrows each on its own. A duplicate by two doers: whoever posed first leads and checks the outcome by reading; the second names what must move over from its node, reads it in the live one and only then closes its own — mutual yielding erases both records. True but no longer held → **release** (`visarjana`), by the holder. Rejected because "we never do it this way" → crystallize the rule with `arose_from` to the rejected question.

**Keep the meaning before letting the form go** (得意忘象, "the meaning grasped, the image is forgotten"). An exposition, a working schema, a seed, a draft is the form a meaning was held in. Once the meaning stands as its own node that a kriya applies, the form is released (`visarjana`), not deleted: it was needed, and history keeps it. Releasing is its own act after the meaning is caught; no such node yet → too early. A schema rescued by ever finer exceptions has lost its meaning — go back to the meaning, don't mend the schema.

## Forbidden triads

422: `badhita + vartamana` (ask "was it ever so?" → `atita`/`vikalpa`, or epistemic back to `anumita`/`kalpita`); `vikalpa + chanda` (move to `anagata`, or will to `upeksha`/`adhimoksha`); `nashta + chanda` (`anagata` anew, or `visarjana`). `badhita + anagata` is allowed, but a refuted plan that never started is `anagata` + `visarjana`.

Warnings: `kalpita + pramanita`. Kind-dependent: Pm + Ag is suspicious for `ding`/`sachverhalt`, fine for `grundsatz`. `nashta` belongs to ontically carried kinds, defect vimarshas (counterexample, grounding defect, drift) among them — gone with their carrier; on a concept, seed, principle, bianhua and will-carried vimarshas `nashta` is a kind error, as is `upeksha` on a bianhua. The surface carries the canon only in part: where it diverges here or in the moves, carry out its refusal or warning as given; where it is silent, hold the canon; report the divergence as feedback (**feedback**) — never choose silently.
